## 📱 IN-PERSON SCRIPTS

### Script 1: At Project Delivery
```
"I'm so glad you're happy with how these [business cards/banners] turned out! 

Before I go, I have a small favor to ask. We're a local, family-owned business, and online reviews really help other Bay Area companies discover our services. 

If you have just 30 seconds, would you mind leaving us a quick review on Google? I can send you the direct link right now."

[If yes]: "Perfect! I'll text you the link right now. Thank you so much!"
[If hesitant]: "No pressure at all! Here's my card with the link if you think of it later."
```

### Script 2: During Project Approval
```
"Great! I'm so excited you love the design. We'll have these printed and ready by [date].

You know, we've been serving Bay Area businesses for [X years], and word-of-mouth is still our best advertising. If you end up being happy with the final product, would you consider leaving us a review online? It really helps other local businesses find us."

[Get commitment]: "Awesome! I'll include the review links with your delivery."
```

```
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from GetBeSeen. I wanted to follow up on your [project] - how did everything turn out?
```

## 📊 REVIEW REQUEST TIMING GUIDE

### Optimal Timing for Review Requests:

**Email Requests**:
- **Best time**: Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 2 PM
- **Avoid**: Monday mornings, Friday afternoons, weekends
- **Follow-up**: Send reminder after 1 week if no response

**Text Requests**:
- **Best time**: Tuesday-Thursday, 9 AM - 5 PM
- **Avoid**: Early mornings, late evenings, weekends
- **Keep it brief**: Under 160 characters

**In-Person Requests**:
- **Best time**: During project delivery or pickup
- **When client is happy**: Right after positive feedback
- **Follow-up**: Send link within 24 hours

### Review Request Sequence:

**Day 0**: Project completion
**Day 1**: Thank you email with subtle review mention
**Day 3**: Direct review request email
**Day 7**: Follow-up email (if no review)
**Day 14**: Final follow-up with incentive (if no review)

## 🎯 PLATFORM-SPECIFIC STRATEGIES

### Google My Business Reviews:
- **Priority**: Focus here first (most important for local SEO)
- **Ask for**: "Google review" specifically
- **Link**: Use direct review link (shorter, easier)
- **Response**: Always respond publicly and professionally

### Yelp Reviews:
- **Approach**: More casual, relationship-focused
- **Ask for**: "Yelp review" or "online review"
- **Timing**: After establishing Google presence
- **Response**: Engage naturally, avoid overly promotional language

### Facebook Reviews:
- **Strategy**: Leverage social connections
- **Ask for**: Reviews from clients who follow your page
- **Content**: More personal, story-driven responses
- **Sharing**: Share positive reviews as posts

## 📈 REVIEW GENERATION CAMPAIGN

### 30-Day Review Blitz Campaign:

**Week 1**: Past Clients Outreach
- Email all clients from past 6 months
- Use Template 1 (Friendly Follow-Up)
- Target: 5-10 reviews

**Week 2**: Recent Clients Follow-Up  
- Contact clients from past 30 days
- Use in-person scripts during deliveries
- Target: 3-5 reviews

**Week 3**: Incentive Campaign
- Offer discount for reviews
- Use Template 2 (Value-First Approach)  
- Target: 3-5 reviews

**Week 4**: VIP Client Outreach
- Contact your best/largest clients
- Personal phone calls or meetings
- Target: 2-3 high-quality reviews

**Goal**: 15-20 reviews in 30 days across all platforms

## 🚨 REVIEW COLLECTION BEST PRACTICES

### DO:
✅ Ask at the right moment (when client is happy)
✅ Make it easy (provide direct links)
✅ Be genuine and personal
✅ Follow up appropriately
✅ Thank reviewers publicly and privately
✅ Respond to all reviews professionally
✅ Track your review generation efforts

### DON'T:
❌ Ask for only 5-star reviews
❌ Offer payment for reviews
❌ Create fake reviews
❌ Pressure or harass clients
❌ Ignore negative reviews
❌ Copy/paste the same response to every review
❌ Ask friends/family for fake reviews
