Frequently Asked Questions

Is PVC board suitable for food service areas?

Yes! PVC board is food-safe, easy to clean, and resistant to grease and cleaning chemicals commonly used in food service.

How do I clean PVC board signs?

Simply wipe with soap and water or standard cleaning products. PVC is chemical resistant and won't stain or degrade.

Can PVC board be used outdoors?

Yes, PVC board is weather resistant and suitable for outdoor use, especially in covered or shaded areas.

Technical Specifications

Material Properties

  • Material: Rigid PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride)
  • Thickness: 3mm, 6mm, 10mm
  • Colors: White, black, colors available
  • Density: 0.55 g/cm³
  • Temperature Range: -10°F to 140°F
