Frequently Asked Questions

Will magnets damage my vehicle's paint?

No, our magnets are safe for automotive paint. However, we recommend removing them weekly for cleaning to prevent dirt buildup.

What's the maximum speed for magnet safety?

Our magnets are tested for highway speeds up to 70+ mph when properly applied to clean, flat metal surfaces.

Do magnets work on all vehicles?

Magnets only work on steel surfaces. Many newer vehicles have aluminum or plastic body panels where magnets won't adhere.

How long do vehicle magnets last?

With proper care, our vehicle magnets typically last 3-5 years of regular outdoor use.
