Frequently Asked Questions

Is styrene suitable for outdoor use?

Styrene is primarily designed for indoor use. For outdoor applications, we recommend PVC, aluminum, or acrylic materials.

Can styrene be cut to custom shapes?

Yes! Styrene cuts cleanly and can be fabricated into virtually any shape or size for custom applications.

What mounting options work with styrene?

Styrene works with adhesive mounting, standoffs, frames, or simple screw mounting depending on the application.

Technical Specifications

Material Properties

  • Material: High-impact polystyrene
  • Color: White (both sides)
  • Thickness: 0.060", 0.080", 0.125"
  • Surface: Smooth, matte finish
  • Temperature Range: 32°F to 140°F

Printing Specifications

  • Print Method: UV flatbed printing
  • Resolution: Up to 1440 DPI
  • Ink Adhesion: Excellent on smooth surface
  • Finish Options: Matte or gloss
  • Indoor Durability: 3+ years
