Frequently Asked Questions

What thicknesses do you offer?

We offer acrylic in various thicknesses including 1/8", 1/4", 3/8", and 1/2" to meet different application needs and budget requirements.

Are acrylic signs suitable for outdoor use?

Yes! Our acrylic is UV-stabilized and weather-resistant, making it perfect for both indoor and outdoor applications.

What printing methods do you use?

We use UV printing for vibrant, durable graphics that won't fade or peel over time.
