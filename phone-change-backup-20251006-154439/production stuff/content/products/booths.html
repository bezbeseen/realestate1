Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide setup services?

Yes! We offer installation services at major trade show venues, or provide detailed setup instructions for self-installation.

How portable are the displays?

Most displays pack into wheeled cases that meet airline requirements and can be set up by 1-2 people in under 30 minutes.

Can graphics be updated?

Yes! Most systems allow graphics to be easily swapped out for different events or updated messaging.

Do you offer rental options?

Yes, we offer rental programs for companies that exhibit infrequently or want to test different booth configurations.
