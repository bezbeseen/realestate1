Frequently Asked Questions

How long do aluminum signs last outdoors?

With proper installation, aluminum signs typically last 10-15 years outdoors with minimal maintenance required.

What mounting options are available?

We offer various mounting options including screw holes, standoffs, brackets, and post mounting systems.

Can aluminum signs be recycled?

Yes! Aluminum is 100% recyclable and retains its properties through multiple recycling cycles.
