Frequently Asked Questions

What paper weights do you offer?

We offer 80lb text (lightweight), 100lb text (standard), and 100lb cover (premium heavyweight) for a substantial feel.

Can you help with design and layout?

Yes! Our graphic design team can create professional brochure layouts, or we offer templates you can customize with your content.

What's the difference between coating options?

Gloss coating provides vibrant colors and protection, matte offers an elegant non-reflective finish, and uncoated allows for easy writing.

How should I prepare my files?

Provide print-ready PDFs with 0.125" bleed, CMYK colors, and fonts outlined. We also accept high-resolution JPEGs and design files.
