Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum order quantity?

Minimum orders vary by pen style: Plastic pens (100 pieces), Metal pens (50 pieces), Premium pens (25 pieces).

What imprint methods do you offer?

We offer screen printing, pad printing, engraving, laser engraving, and full-color digital printing depending on the pen style.

How long do the pens write?

Our standard pens write for approximately 1-2 miles of writing, depending on the ink type and writing pressure.

Can I get a sample before ordering?

Yes! We provide samples for most pen styles so you can feel the quality before placing your order.
