Frequently Asked Questions

What's the largest size you can print?

We can print posters up to 60" wide with virtually unlimited length, perfect for building wraps and large venue displays.

Can posters be laminated for protection?

Yes! We offer matte or gloss lamination to protect against moisture, UV rays, and handling damage for long-term display.

What resolution do I need for large posters?

For viewing distances over 3 feet, 150 DPI is sufficient. For close viewing, we recommend 300 DPI for crisp detail.

Do you offer mounting options?

Yes! We can mount posters to foam board, gator board, or aluminum for rigid display solutions.
