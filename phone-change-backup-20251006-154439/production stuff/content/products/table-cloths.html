Frequently Asked Questions

What table sizes do you accommodate?

We offer standard sizes for 6-foot and 8-foot tables, as well as custom sizes for any table dimensions.

Are the table cloths machine washable?

Yes! Our table cloths are machine washable in cold water and can be air dried or tumble dried on low heat.

How long does production take?

Standard production time is 7-10 business days. Rush orders available in 3-5 business days for additional fees.

Do you offer flame retardant options?

Yes, we offer flame retardant fabrics that meet trade show and convention center requirements.
