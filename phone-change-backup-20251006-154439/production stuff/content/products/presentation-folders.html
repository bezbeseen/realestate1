Frequently Asked Questions

What paper stock do you recommend?

We recommend 14pt or 16pt cardstock for durability. For premium presentations, 18pt with UV coating provides exceptional quality.

Can you add special finishes?

Yes! We offer foil stamping, embossing, spot UV, and soft-touch coatings to make your folders stand out.

How much can the pockets hold?

Standard pockets hold 15-20 sheets of paper comfortably. Reinforced pockets can accommodate up to 25-30 sheets.

Do you offer custom sizes?

Absolutely! We can create folders in any size to accommodate your specific documents and branding needs.
