Frequently Asked Questions

What thickness options are available?

Standard thicknesses range from 1/4" to 2". Custom thicknesses available for special applications up to 4".

Can letters be illuminated?

Yes! We offer LED backlighting, edge lighting, and face-lit options for stunning nighttime visibility.

What's the largest size you can make?

Individual letters can be made up to 8 feet tall. Larger installations use multiple panels joined seamlessly.

Do you provide installation services?

Yes! Our certified installers handle everything from wall preparation to final positioning and electrical connections.

Technical Specifications

Material Properties

  • Foam Core: 1/4" to 2" thickness
  • Acrylic: 1/8" to 1" clear or colored
  • Aluminum: .040" to .125" thickness
  • Stainless Steel: .060" to .125" thickness
  • PVC: 1/4" to 1" expanded or solid

Finishing Options

  • Paint: Any color match available
  • Vinyl Graphics: Digital print application
  • Brushed Finish: Directional metal texture
  • Powder Coating: Durable colored finish
  • LED Integration: Internal or external lighting
