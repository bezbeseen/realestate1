Frequently Asked Questions

How long do LED channel letters last?

Our LED channel letters are designed to last 50,000+ hours (5-7 years of continuous operation) with minimal maintenance required.

Do you handle permits and installation?

Yes! We provide full-service installation including permit applications, electrical work, and professional mounting.

What sizes are available?

Channel letters can be made in virtually any size, typically ranging from 6" to 48" in height depending on visibility requirements and local codes.

Can existing signs be converted to LED?

In many cases, yes! We can retrofit existing channel letters with new LED lighting systems for improved efficiency and brightness.
