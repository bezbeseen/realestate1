Frequently Asked Questions

How long do LED lights last?

Our LED systems are rated for 50,000+ hours of operation - approximately 15+ years of typical use.

Can graphics be changed easily?

Yes! Snap-frame systems allow graphic changes in under 30 seconds without tools. Perfect for rotating promotions.

Are they suitable for outdoor use?

We offer weatherproof models with IP65 rating for outdoor applications, including temperature compensation.

What power requirements do they have?

Most units operate on standard 110V power. Large installations may require dedicated circuits for optimal performance.
