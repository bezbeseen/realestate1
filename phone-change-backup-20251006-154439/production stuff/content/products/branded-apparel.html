Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum order quantity?

Minimum orders vary by customization method: Embroidery (12 pieces), Screen Printing (24 pieces), Heat Transfer (6 pieces).

How long does production take?

Standard orders: 7-10 business days. Rush orders: 3-5 business days available for additional fees.

Do you provide sizing samples?

Yes, we can provide sizing samples for most garments to ensure proper fit for your team.
