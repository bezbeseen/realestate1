Frequently Asked Questions

How long does setup take?

Our pop-up tents can be set up by two people in under 5 minutes with no tools required.

Are the tents waterproof?

The canopy is water-resistant and will handle light rain, but we recommend additional protection for heavy weather.

What's included with the tent?

Each tent includes the aluminum frame, custom printed canopy, stakes, guy ropes, and a wheeled carrying case.

Can I get sidewalls?

Yes! We offer optional sidewalls with or without windows, and they can also be custom printed.

Technical Specifications

Frame Specifications

  • Material: Powder-coated aluminum
  • Tube Diameter: 40mm square legs
  • Weight: 35-55 lbs depending on size
  • Height: Adjustable 8'6" to 9'6"
  • Wind Rating: 25 mph with proper anchoring

Canopy Specifications

  • Material: 600D polyester fabric
  • Print Method: Dye sublimation
  • UV Rating: UPF 50+ sun protection
