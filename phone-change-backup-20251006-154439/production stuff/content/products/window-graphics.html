Frequently Asked Questions

How long do window graphics last?

High-quality window graphics last 5-7 years outdoors and 10+ years indoors with proper installation and care.

Can they be removed without damage?

Yes! Professional-grade adhesives allow clean removal without damaging glass or leaving residue when properly heated.

Do you offer installation services?

Absolutely! Our certified installers ensure perfect application with no bubbles, wrinkles, or alignment issues.

What about building codes and permits?

We're familiar with local codes regarding window coverage percentages and can ensure your graphics meet all requirements.

Technical Specifications

Material Properties

  • Vinyl Film: 3.2-4.0 mil cast or calendered
  • Perforated Vinyl: 50/50 or 60/40 see-through ratios
  • Adhesive: Removable or permanent options
  • UV Resistance: 5-7 year outdoor rating
  • Temperature Range: -40°F to 180°F

Installation Specifications

  • Surface Prep: Clean with alcohol-based solution
  • Application Temp: 65-75°F optimal range
  • Squeegee Pressure: Firm, overlapping strokes
  • Edge Sealing: Primer for long-term adhesion
  • Cure Time: 48-72 hours for full bond strength
