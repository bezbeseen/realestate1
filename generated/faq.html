Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

What printing services do you offer in Santa Clara?

We offer comprehensive printing services including business cards, banners, signs, promotional materials, real estate marketing materials, and large format printing. Our services cover everything from design to production and delivery throughout Silicon Valley.

How long does it take to print business cards?

Standard business card printing takes 2-3 business days. We also offer same-day rush service for urgent orders. Premium finishes like foil stamping or embossing may take 3-5 business days.

Do you offer design services for printing projects?

Yes! We provide full graphic design services including logo design, branding, layout design, and photo editing. Our design team works with you to create professional materials that represent your business effectively.

What is your minimum order quantity for business cards?

Our minimum order for business cards is 100 cards. However, we recommend ordering 500-1000 cards for better pricing and to ensure you have enough for your business needs.

Do you deliver to other cities in Silicon Valley?

Yes! We deliver throughout Silicon Valley including San Jose, Mountain View, Palo Alto, Sunnyvale, Cupertino, Campbell, Los Gatos, Saratoga, and Fremont. Free delivery is available for orders over $200.

What file formats do you accept for printing?

We accept all major file formats including PDF, AI, PSD, EPS, PNG, and JPG. PDF files are preferred for best results. We can also work with Microsoft Word and PowerPoint files if needed.

Do you offer rush printing services?

Yes! We offer same-day and next-day rush services for most printing projects. Rush orders may have additional fees, but we understand that sometimes you need materials quickly for important meetings or events.

What makes your printing services different from competitors?

With 25+ years serving Silicon Valley, we combine traditional printing expertise with modern technology. We specialize in real estate marketing materials, understand the tech industry, and provide personalized service with competitive pricing and fast turnaround.

How do I get a quote for my printing project?

Getting a quote is easy! Call us at (669) 272-2682, email us at contact@getbeseen.com, or visit our Santa Clara location at 377 Laurelwood Road. We provide free quotes and design consultations for all projects.

Do you offer bulk discounts for large orders?

Yes! We offer volume discounts for large orders. The more you order, the better the pricing. Contact us with your quantity requirements and we'll provide competitive bulk pricing for your printing needs.
