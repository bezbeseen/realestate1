## 🎯 REVIEW GENERATION STRATEGY

### Getting Your First Reviews:

**Phase 1: Existing Clients**
1. **Identify happy clients** from past 6 months
2. **Send personalized emails** requesting reviews
3. **Provide direct link** to your Yelp page
4. **Follow up** if no response after 1 week

**Email Template**:
```
Subject: Quick favor - would you mind sharing your experience?

Hi [Client Name],

Hope you're doing well! We were thrilled to help you with [specific project] recently, and I wanted to reach out with a small favor.

Would you mind taking 2 minutes to share your experience on Yelp? Your feedback helps other Bay Area businesses discover our services and means the world to our small team.

Here's the direct link: [Your Yelp URL]

Thanks so much for your business and for considering this request!

Best regards,
[Your Name]
GetBeSeen Team
```

**Phase 2: New Clients**
- **Ask at project completion**: "If you're happy with the results, would you mind leaving us a review?"
- **Include in follow-up emails**: Add review request to project completion emails
- **Business cards**: Include QR code linking to Yelp page

### Review Response Strategy:

**5-Star Reviews**:
```
Thank you so much for the wonderful review, [Name]! We're thrilled you were happy with your [project type]. It was a pleasure working with you, and we look forward to helping with your future printing needs. Thanks for choosing GetBeSeen!
```

**4-Star Reviews**:
```
Thanks for the great review, [Name]! We're glad you were pleased with your [project]. We appreciate the feedback and are always looking for ways to improve our service. Please don't hesitate to reach out for future projects!
```

**3-Star or Lower Reviews**:
```
Thank you for your feedback, [Name]. I'm sorry your experience didn't meet expectations. I'd love to discuss this further and see how we can make it right. Please call me directly at (669) 273-1583 or email contact@getbeseen.com. We value your business and want to ensure you're completely satisfied.
```

## 🚨 YELP BEST PRACTICES

### DO:
✅ Respond to ALL reviews (positive and negative)
✅ Keep business information up-to-date
✅ Post high-quality, relevant photos regularly
✅ Use natural keywords in your description
✅ Encourage satisfied customers to leave reviews
✅ Monitor your Yelp page daily
✅ Engage with the local business community

### DON'T:
❌ Ask for only 5-star reviews
❌ Offer incentives for reviews (against Yelp policy)
❌ Ignore negative reviews
❌ Use fake or stock photos
❌ Stuff your description with keywords
❌ Create fake reviews or ask friends/family
❌ Get defensive with negative feedback
