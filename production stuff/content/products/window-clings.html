Window Clings - Removable Glass Graphics

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do window clings last?

Indoor window clings can last 2-3 years. Outdoor applications typically last 6-12 months depending on weather exposure.

What surfaces do they stick to?

Window clings work best on smooth, non-porous surfaces like glass, mirrors, and glossy plastics. They won't stick to textured surfaces.

Can they be printed on both sides?

Yes! Double-sided printing is available for window displays visible from both inside and outside.

How do I clean and maintain them?

Clean with mild soap and water, then rinse thoroughly. Allow to air dry completely before reapplying.

Technical Specifications

Material Properties

  • Base Material: 4 mil static cling vinyl
  • Adhesive: None - uses static electricity

Printing Specifications

  • Print Method: Digital UV printing
