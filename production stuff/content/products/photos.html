Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats do you accept?

We accept JPEG, TIFF, PNG, and RAW files. For best quality, provide high-resolution files in sRGB color space.

Can you print from phone photos?

Yes! Modern smartphone cameras produce excellent results for prints up to 8x10. Larger sizes depend on the specific phone model.

Do you offer color correction?

Our automatic color correction is included. Professional color correction and retouching services are available for an additional fee.

How long will my prints last?

Our archival papers and inks are rated for 100+ years when stored properly, ensuring your memories last for generations.
