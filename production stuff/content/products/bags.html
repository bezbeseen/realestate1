Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum order quantity?

Minimum orders vary by bag type: Non-woven bags (100 pieces), Canvas bags (50 pieces), Premium bags (25 pieces).

What printing methods do you offer?

We offer screen printing, heat transfer, embroidery, and digital printing depending on the bag material and design requirements.

Are your bags eco-friendly?

Yes! We offer organic cotton, recycled materials, and biodegradable options for environmentally conscious brands.
