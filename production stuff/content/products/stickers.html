Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between stickers and decals?

Stickers typically have a white or colored background, while decals are often transparent or die-cut to follow the shape of your design without extra background material.

Can stickers be used outdoors?

Yes! Our vinyl stickers are weather-resistant and UV-protected, lasting 3-5 years outdoors. Paper stickers are best for indoor use only.

What's the smallest size you can print?

We can print stickers as small as 0.5" x 0.5", though we recommend at least 1" for text readability and detailed graphics.

Do you offer waterproof stickers?

Yes! Our vinyl stickers with UV lamination are completely waterproof and perfect for water bottles, outdoor equipment, and marine applications.
