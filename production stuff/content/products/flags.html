Frequently Asked Questions

How long do outdoor flags last?

Our flags are made from durable polyester and typically last 6-12 months outdoors depending on weather conditions and usage.

What's included with flag orders?

Each flag includes the printed fabric, pole system, ground stake or base, and carrying bag for easy transport and storage.

Can flags withstand wind?

Our flags are designed for outdoor use and can handle normal wind conditions. For high-wind areas, we offer reinforced options and weighted bases.

Do you offer indoor base options?

Yes! We offer water-fillable bases and weighted stands for indoor use where ground stakes aren't suitable.
