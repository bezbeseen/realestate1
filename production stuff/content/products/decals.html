Frequently Asked Questions

How long do vinyl decals last outdoors?

Our premium vinyl decals are rated for 5-7 years of outdoor use, depending on climate conditions and UV exposure.

Can decals be removed without damage?

Yes! Our decals use removable adhesive that won't damage paint or surfaces when removed properly with heat.

Do you offer custom shapes and sizes?

Absolutely! We can die-cut decals to any shape or size, including complex designs and logos.

What surfaces work best for decals?

Decals adhere best to smooth, clean surfaces like glass, metal, painted surfaces, and high-quality plastics.

Technical Specifications

Material Properties

  • Base Material: 3.2 mil premium vinyl
  • Adhesive: Permanent or removable options
  • Finish: Gloss, matte, or textured
  • Temperature Range: -40°F to 180°F
  • UV Resistance: Rated for outdoor use

Printing Specifications

  • Print Method: Solvent or eco-solvent digital
  • Resolution: Up to 1440 DPI
  • Color Gamut: Full CMYK + spot colors
  • Lamination: Optional protective overlaminate
  • Contour Cut: Precision die-cutting available

Application Guidelines

  • Surface Prep: Clean with alcohol-based cleaner, ensure dry
  • Application Temperature: 60-80°F for best results
  • Installation Tools: Squeegee and heat gun recommended
  • Cure Time: 24-48 hours for full adhesion strength
