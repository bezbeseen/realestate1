Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change the graphics?

Graphics can be easily swapped by removing the rail, sliding out the old banner, and inserting the new one. The process takes just a few minutes.

What's included with each banner stand?

Each stand includes the aluminum base, telescoping pole, printed banner, and a padded carrying case with wheels.

How stable are retractable banners?

Our premium bases are weighted and stable for indoor use. For outdoor use, we recommend additional weights or ground stakes.

Can I get replacement graphics?

Yes! We can print replacement graphics for any of our banner stands, making them a great long-term investment.
