Corrugated Plastic Signs - Durable Outdoor Signage

Our corrugated plastic signs deliver exceptional durability and weather resistance for outdoor applications. Made from twin-wall polypropylene, these signs are lightweight yet incredibly strong, making them perfect for temporary and semi-permanent outdoor signage that needs to withstand the elements.

Key Advantages:

  • Weather Resistant: Won't warp, crack, or fade
  • Lightweight: Easy to transport and install
  • Cost-Effective: Affordable outdoor signage solution
  • Durable: Impact resistant twin-wall construction
  • Versatile: Easy to cut, drill, and fabricate

Perfect Applications:

  • Real Estate Yard Signs
  • Construction Site Signs
  • Political Campaign Signs
  • Event Directional Signs
  • Temporary Advertising
  • Property Management Signs

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do corrugated signs last outdoors?

With proper installation, corrugated plastic signs typically last 2-3 years outdoors with minimal fading or degradation.

Can corrugated plastic be recycled?

Yes! Corrugated plastic is recyclable and environmentally friendly compared to other outdoor signage materials.

What mounting options are available?

We offer H-stakes, wire stands, screw mounting, and adhesive mounting options depending on your installation needs.
