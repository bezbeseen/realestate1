Frequently Asked Questions

How long do yard signs last outdoors?

Corrugated plastic yard signs typically last 6-12 months outdoors, while aluminum signs can last 2-3 years with proper care.

What mounting options are included?

Standard wire stakes are included with corrugated signs. We also offer heavy-duty stands, posts, and frames for different applications.

Can I get double-sided printing?

Yes! Double-sided printing is available for maximum visibility from both directions.

Do you offer quantity discounts?

Yes, we offer significant discounts for quantities over 25, 50, 100, and 500 pieces for campaigns and bulk orders.

Material Options

  • Corrugated Plastic: 4mm lightweight, economical
  • Aluminum: .040" for long-term use
  • Foam Board: 3/16" for indoor/short-term
  • Magnetic: 30mil for vehicle placement

Printing Specifications

  • Print Method: UV-resistant digital printing
