Frequently Asked Questions

How long do foam board signs last?

Indoor use: 2-3 years, Outdoor use: 6-12 months depending on weather conditions and mounting method.

Can foam board be used outdoors?

Yes, but we recommend thicker boards (1/2" or 3/4") and proper mounting for outdoor durability.

What mounting options do you offer?

We offer stands, easels, wall mounts, and custom mounting solutions for any application.

Technical Specifications

Material Properties

  • Core Material: Expanded polystyrene (EPS)
  • Surface: White paper or vinyl
  • Thickness: 3/16", 1/2", 3/4"
  • Weight: 1-3 lbs per square foot
  • Temperature Range: 32°F to 120°F

Printing Specifications

  • Print Method: UV printing
  • Resolution: Up to 1440 DPI
  • Color Gamut: Full CMYK
  • Maximum Size: 4' x 8'
  • Finish: Matte or gloss
