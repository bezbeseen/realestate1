Frequently Asked Questions

How long do canvas prints last?

Our archival inks and UV-resistant coatings ensure canvas prints last 75-100 years without significant fading when displayed indoors.

What resolution do I need for large canvases?

For optimal quality, we recommend 150-300 DPI depending on viewing distance. We can advise on file requirements for your specific size.

Can you color-correct my photos?

Yes! Our color specialists can enhance and correct photos to ensure the best possible canvas reproduction.

Do you offer protective coatings?

We apply UV-resistant coatings as standard. Additional protective varnishes are available for high-traffic areas.
