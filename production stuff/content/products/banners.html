Vinyl Banner Options

Our custom vinyl banners are the perfect solution for advertising your business, event, or promotion. Made from durable, weather-resistant materials, they are suitable for both indoor and outdoor use. We offer a variety of materials and finishing options to meet your specific needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What materials are your banners made from?

Our banners are made from premium 13oz vinyl material that's weather-resistant, UV-protected, and designed to last 3-5 years outdoors.

Can banners be used outdoors?

Yes! Our vinyl banners are specifically designed for outdoor use with UV-resistant inks and weather-resistant materials that withstand rain, wind, and sun exposure.

What finishing options are available?

We offer grommets every 2 feet, pole pockets (top, bottom, or both), and reinforced hems. Custom finishing options are available upon request.

How do I hang my banner?

Banners with grommets can be hung with zip ties, bungee cords, or rope. Pole pocket banners slide onto poles or rods for easy installation.

What's the largest size you can print?

We can print banners up to 16 feet wide with virtually unlimited length. For larger installations, we can create multiple panels that align perfectly.

Do you offer double-sided printing?

Yes! Double-sided banners are available with blockout material between layers to prevent show-through, perfect for hanging installations.

Technical Specifications

Material Properties

  • Base Material: 13oz scrim vinyl
  • Finish: Matte or gloss options
  • Weight: 13 oz per square yard
  • Thickness: 15 mil
  • Temperature Range: -40°F to 180°F
  • Wind Resistance: Up to 75 mph with proper installation

Printing Specifications

  • Print Method: Large format digital printing
  • Resolution: 720-1440 DPI
  • Color Profile: Full CMYK process
  • Ink Type: UV-resistant eco-solvent
  • Color Matching: Pantone colors available
  • Fade Resistance: 3-5 years outdoor

Finishing Options

Grommets

Nickel-plated grommets every 2 feet around perimeter with reinforced stress points.

Pole Pockets

2" or 3" pole pockets on top, bottom, or both sides for easy hanging.

Reinforced Hems

Double-folded and heat-welded hems for maximum durability and tear resistance.
