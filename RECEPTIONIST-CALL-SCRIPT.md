# Receptionist Call Script - BE SEEN Print & Sign

## 📞 Professional Call Handling Script

### **Initial Greeting (Warm & Professional)**
> "Thank you for calling BE SEEN Print and Sign. This is [Your Name], how may I help you today?"

**Variations:**
- "Good morning/afternoon! You've reached BE SEEN Print and Sign. This is [Your Name], how can I assist you?"
- "BE SEEN Print and Sign, [Your Name] speaking. How may I help you today?"

---

## 🎯 **Customer Intent Detection Questions**

### **Primary Question (Ask First):**
> "Are you calling about a specific project you'd like to get started on, or are you looking for information about our services?"

### **Follow-up Questions Based on Response:**

**If they have a specific project:**
> "Wonderful! What type of project are you working on?"

**If they want information:**
> "What type of printing or signage services are you most interested in learning about?"

---

## 📋 **Service Categories & Key Questions**

### **🖨️ PRINTING SERVICES**
**Questions to Ask:**
- "What type of printing do you need? Business cards, flyers, brochures, banners, or something else?"
- "How many pieces do you need?"
- "What's your timeline for this project?"
- "Do you have your artwork ready, or do you need design services as well?"

**Key Information to Gather:**
- Quantity needed
- Size specifications
- Paper/finish preferences
- Design requirements
- Timeline/deadline

### **🏢 SIGNAGE SERVICES**
**Questions to Ask:**
- "What type of signage are you looking for? Indoor, outdoor, vehicle graphics, or window graphics?"
- "Is this for a business, event, or real estate purposes?"
- "Do you need installation services as well?"
- "What's the size and location of where this will be displayed?"

**Key Information to Gather:**
- Sign type and size
- Installation requirements
- Location details
- Timeline for completion

### **🏠 REAL ESTATE SERVICES**
**Questions to Ask:**
- "Are you a real estate agent looking for marketing materials?"
- "What type of real estate signs do you need? Lawn signs, A-frames, or directional signs?"
- "How many properties are you marketing?"
- "Do you need installation and removal services?"

### **📦 PROMOTIONAL PRODUCTS**
**Questions to Ask:**
- "What type of promotional items are you interested in? Apparel, bags, pens, or branded merchandise?"
- "What's the quantity you're looking for?"
- "What's your budget range for this project?"

---

## 💰 **Pricing & Quote Process**

### **When Customer Asks About Pricing:**
> "I'd be happy to get you a quote! Let me gather some details about your project so I can give you the most accurate pricing."

**Then ask:**
1. **Quantity needed**
2. **Size specifications**
3. **Material preferences** (if applicable)
4. **Timeline requirements**
5. **Design needs**

### **Quote Delivery Options:**
> "I can get you a detailed quote within the next business day. Would you prefer that via email, or would you like me to call you back?"

**If they need it urgently:**
> "I can get you a rush quote today. Let me put you on hold for just a moment while I check our current pricing and availability."

---

## 📞 **Lead Qualification Questions**

### **Business Information:**
- "What's the name of your business?"
- "Are you the decision maker for this project?"
- "What's the best way to reach you for follow-up?"
- "Do you have an email address where I can send the quote?"

### **Timeline Assessment:**
- "When do you need this project completed?"
- "Is this for a specific event or deadline?"
- "Are you flexible on the timeline if we can offer better pricing?"

### **Budget Understanding:**
- "Do you have a budget range in mind for this project?"
- "Are you comparing quotes from other vendors?"

---

## 🎨 **Design Services Upsell**

### **When Customer Needs Design:**
> "Great! We offer professional design services as well. Our design team can create custom artwork for your project. Would you like me to include design services in your quote?"

**Design Service Questions:**
- "Do you have any existing branding or logos we should use?"
- "What style or look are you going for?"
- "Do you have any reference materials or inspiration?"

---

## 🚀 **Rush Job Handling**

### **When Customer Needs Rush Service:**
> "I understand you're on a tight timeline. Let me check our current production schedule to see what we can accommodate."

**Rush Job Information:**
- Rush jobs typically have a 25-50% premium
- We can often accommodate 24-48 hour turnarounds for simple projects
- Complex projects may need 3-5 business days minimum

### **Rush Job Response:**
> "I can definitely help with that timeline. Rush orders do have a premium, but we can get your project done quickly. Let me get you a quote that includes the rush pricing."

---

## 📍 **Service Area Questions**

### **Location-Based Services:**
> "What city are you located in? We serve the entire Bay Area and can provide delivery and installation services."

**Service Areas:**
- Santa Clara (our main location)
- San Jose, Palo Alto, Sunnyvale, Mountain View
- Cupertino, Campbell, Los Gatos, Saratoga
- Fremont and surrounding Bay Area

### **Delivery/Installation:**
- "Do you need delivery or installation services?"
- "Is this for a business address or residential?"
- "Are there any access restrictions we should know about?"

---

## 📧 **Follow-up Process**

### **Quote Follow-up:**
> "I'll have your quote ready by [specific time]. What's the best number to reach you at, and is there a preferred time to call?"

### **Project Follow-up:**
> "Once you receive the quote, feel free to call back with any questions. We're here to help make your project successful!"

---

## 🚫 **Objection Handling**

### **"Your prices are too high":**
> "I understand budget is important. Let me see if we can adjust the specifications or quantity to better fit your budget. What's your target price range?"

### **"I need to think about it":**
> "Of course! Take your time to review the quote. I'll follow up in a few days to answer any questions you might have."

### **"I'm comparing with other vendors":**
> "That's smart to compare options. We're confident in our quality and service. What specific factors are most important to you in making this decision?"

---

## 📝 **Call Documentation**

### **Information to Record:**
- Customer name and business name
- Phone number and email address
- Project details and specifications
- Timeline requirements
- Budget range (if provided)
- Follow-up date and time
- Any special requirements or notes

### **Quote Request Process:**
1. Gather all project details
2. Confirm contact information
3. Set expectation for quote delivery
4. Schedule follow-up if needed
5. Thank them for calling BE SEEN

---

### **Information Request:**
> "I hope I've answered all your questions. Feel free to call back anytime, or visit our website at getbeseen.com for more information. Thank you for calling BE SEEN!"

### **Future Follow-up:**
> "I'll make sure to follow up with you on [date/time]. In the meantime, if you have any other questions, don't hesitate to call. Thank you for your interest in BE SEEN Print and Sign!"

## 📞 **Emergency/After Hours**

### **After Hours Message:**
> "Thank you for calling BE SEEN Print and Sign. Our regular business hours are Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. For urgent projects, please leave a detailed message with your name, phone number, project details, and timeline. We'll return your call on the next business day."

---

## 💡 **Pro Tips for Receptionist**

1. **Listen actively** - Let customers explain their needs fully
2. **Ask clarifying questions** - Don't assume what they need
3. **Be enthusiastic** - Show excitement about their project
4. **Take detailed notes** - Every detail matters for accurate quotes
5. **Follow up promptly** - Quick response shows professionalism
6. **Know when to transfer** - Complex technical questions should go to the team
7. **Upsell appropriately** - Suggest additional services that add value
8. **Stay organized** - Use a system to track all quote requests

---

- Number of calls per day

- Customer satisfaction with call experience
